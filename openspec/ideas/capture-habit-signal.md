# Capture habit signal

- **Status:** open
- **Likely capabilities:** contribution, capture
- **ADRs:** None yet.

## Why

Capture has counts but no habit signal, so the writer can't see whether they're catching ideas regularly or letting the inbox grow.

## What could change

Decide whether capture gets its own progress signal, such as ideas per week or time to inbox zero.

## Out of scope / constraints

Must not add any step to capture itself: one keystroke, works on mobile.
