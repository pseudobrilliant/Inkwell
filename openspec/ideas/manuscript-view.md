# Manuscript view

- **Status:** open
- **Likely capabilities:** long-form
- **ADRs:** ADR 0004: check StoryLine's views first.

## Why

There's no way to read all of a project's scenes as one continuous document.

## What could change

A manuscript view for reading a project straight through.

## Out of scope / constraints

Reading only. No compile or export: Inkwell is not a publishing tool.
