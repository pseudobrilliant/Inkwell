# Long-form views

- **Status:** gated by [ADR 0004](../decisions/0004-storyline-compatible-long-form.md)
- **Likely capabilities:** long-form
- **ADRs:** Gated by ADR 0004.

## Why

Corkboard, kanban, beat-sheet scaffolds and a scene inspector have been suggested for long form.

## What could change

Build any of these only if StoryLine falls short in real use. Record the gap here first.

## Out of scope / constraints

Nothing is built until a real gap in StoryLine is written down.
