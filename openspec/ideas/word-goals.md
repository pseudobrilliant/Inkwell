# Daily and weekly word goals

- **Status:** open (after block-word-log)
- **Likely capabilities:** practice-stats, contribution
- **ADRs:** [ADR 0007](../decisions/0007-inkwell-keeps-its-own-word-log.md) (the count it is built on).

## Why

The [constitution](../constitution.md) makes daily and weekly word-count goals core, and makes Home the place progress is seen. Today Inkwell has time goals for exercises and journals and word targets for pieces, but no goal for the day or the week.

## What could change

- Settings for a daily and a weekly word goal.
- A Home block showing today's and this week's words against the goal, with totals.
- A notice, or a mark in the writing block, when the day's goal is reached.

## Open questions

- Does the week's goal follow from the day's (7×, or by writing days), or is it set on its own?
- Do practice and piece words both count toward the day's goal? (The counting rule says practice counts for the day, so likely yes.)
- Does a missed day break a streak, or only a missed weekly goal?

## Out of scope / constraints

Goals encourage, they don't scold: no red marks for a missed day. Goals are the writer's, not the device's, so they live in the synced settings.
