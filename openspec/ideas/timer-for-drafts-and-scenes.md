# Timer for drafts and scenes

- **Status:** open
- **Likely capabilities:** writing-timer, piece-progress
- **ADRs:** ADR 0003 (timer state) still applies.

## Why

The writing timer covers exercises and journals only.

## What could change

Decide whether the timer should time drafts and scenes too, and what the minutes would mean there.

## Out of scope / constraints

Piece progress is measured in words, not minutes. The counting rule (practice never counts toward a piece) must hold.
