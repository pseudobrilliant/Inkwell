# Weekly review

- **Status:** open
- **Likely capabilities:** capture, practice, short-form, piece-progress (or a new weekly-review capability)
- **ADRs:** None yet.

## Why

Nothing ties Capture and the modes together on a regular rhythm. Inboxes fill and stories stall without anyone noticing.

## What could change

A weekly review checklist: inbox zero, picking an exercise, and checking stalled stories.

## Out of scope / constraints

No new tracking. It reads what the other capabilities already record.
