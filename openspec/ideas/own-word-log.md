# Inkwell's own word log

- **Status:** gated by [ADR 0002](../decisions/0002-rely-on-keep-the-rhythm-for-word-logs.md)
- **Likely capabilities:** ktr-integration, practice-stats, piece-progress, contribution
- **ADRs:** Gated by ADR 0002. Doing it means a new ADR that supersedes 0002.

## Why

Keep the Rhythm's per-device history can undercount across phone and desktop.

## What could change

An Inkwell word log, behind the same one-file interface as the KTR integration, only if KTR's history proves unreliable in real use.

## Out of scope / constraints

To be decided.
